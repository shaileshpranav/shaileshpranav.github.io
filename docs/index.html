About Me

Welcome! I'm Shailesh, a Software Engineer deeply passionate about harnessing the power of Generative AI and Machine Learning to create impactful, enterprise-grade solutions.

From Nov 2023 to Jul 2026 I worked at WorldLink US in Frisco, TX as a GenAI / ML Engineer, building multimodal RAG assistants, real-time speech (Whisper, MeloTTS) and optimised LLM serving, and working with client teams on a support assistant and ML pricing pipelines. My background includes a Master of Engineering in Robotics from the University of Maryland, providing a strong foundation in intelligent systems and robust engineering principles.

My focus is on building solutions that not only perform exceptionally well but also prioritize data privacy and security. From developing secure chatbots with role-based access control to architecting Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems across audio, video, and text, I thrive on translating complex requirements into deployable, scalable applications.
